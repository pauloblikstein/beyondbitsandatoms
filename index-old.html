§ 01 · About the studio

A two-week intensive in the design of education products, with a focus on AI tools for making

The BBA Summer Studio is a compressed, hands-on version of Beyond Bits & Atoms, focused on Making with AI tools. Across ten days you'll move through the lab's full toolkit — laser cutter, 3D printers, microcontrollers, woodworking, coding, prototyping — and use them to design and build a working educational technology of your own. We read just enough theory to argue about why we build the way we do, then spend the rest of the day at the bench.

§ 02 · In more detail

What the two weeks actually look like.

The shape of a day

Every day runs 9 to 5 as an open studio. Mornings open with a short circle — a quick reading or a demo — then a long build block. After lunch, a second build block carries the day's work forward, and we close at 4:30 with a share-out where everyone shows what moved.

The first week is a tools-and-techniques tour: by Friday you'll have cut, printed, wired, and programmed something, and pitched three directions for a final project. The second week is yours — you scope a project, build it, put it in front of real users, iterate, and exhibit it at a closing mini-Expo.

What you'll make

Each participant leaves with one working prototype of a constructionist learning technology — something a learner could actually pick up and use — plus a short write-up documenting the idea, the build, and what testing revealed.

Reading & theory

Reading is deliberately light. A handful of short, foundational pieces frame the work — enough to give the building a point of view, never so much that it crowds out time in the lab. Reading list to be finalized.

Who it's for

No prior fabrication or electronics experience is assumed. The studio suits educators, designers, and graduate students who want to think with their hands. Prerequisites & audience to be confirmed.
